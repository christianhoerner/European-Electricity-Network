# ⚡ European Electricity Network: Consequences of Integration

This project explores how electricity is produced, consumed, traded, and priced across Europe — with a special focus on **France, Germany, Belgium, and Italy**.  
The analysis is based on data from **French and European institutions** and is presented in both **interactive dashboards** (Looker Studio) and a **short presentation**.

---

## 🎤 5-Minute Presentation Script

### Slide 1: Introduction
- Central Question: **What are the consequences of a shared European electricity network that we can see from the data?**
- Focus on production, consumption, and trade patterns.
- Countries: France, Germany, Belgium, Italy.

---

### Slide 2: Production & Consumption Balance
- Shared network lets countries consume beyond their own production and export surpluses.
- France: consistently a **net exporter**.  
- Germany + France: dominate EU production and consumption.  
- **Consequence #1:** *Enhanced energy security and balance through sharing*  
  → Production must always equal consumption to avoid blackouts.

---

### Slide 3: Energy Mix Diversity
- Nuclear + Hydro = >40% of EU electricity mix.  
- **Country specializations:**  
  - 🇫🇷 France: Nuclear  
  - 🇩🇪 Germany: Coal + Renewables  
  - 🇳🇴 Nordics: Hydro  
- **Consequence #2:** *Geographic specialization based on natural resources.*

---

### Slide 4: Renewable Energy Transition
- Fossil fuels declining, Solar/Wind increasing across EU.  
- 8-year trend: Nuclear + Coal ↓, Solar + Wind ↑.  
- Shared network enables coordinated clean energy transition.  
- **Consequence #3:** *EU-wide shift toward renewables while maintaining stability.*

---

### Slide 5: Renewables Prediction
- Machine learning models predict the renewable transition will continue.

---

### Slide 6: Electricity Price
- Prices = supply + grid fees + taxes (taxes vary widely).  
- Example: 🇩🇪 Germany → majority of price from fees + taxes.  
- **Key point:** *National policy strongly influences electricity prices.*

---

### Slide 7: France Exports
- France is a consistent exporter (except 2022).  
- State regulations flatten out consumer price fluctuations.  
- **Key Insight:** *Regulation is needed to balance volatile markets.*

---

### Slide 12: Key Takeaways
1. **Security through diversity** – shared resources prevent shortages.  
2. **Specialization by geography** – countries leverage natural advantages.  
3. **Policy independence persists** – national rules still drive price.  
4. **Adaptive infrastructure** – evolving for renewables + EVs.  
5. **Resilience requires balance** – integration brings both benefits and vulnerabilities.

**Conclusion:**  
The shared European electricity network creates *both opportunities and dependencies*, reshaping how we produce, consume, and price electricity.
